How It Works

A Clear Process From First Visit to Final Walkthrough

01

Property Consultation

We review the property, discuss the problem or goal, and determine whether the project fits Blue Back’s services.

02

Scope and Estimate

We define the proposed work, materials, schedule considerations, and pricing in a clear written estimate.

03

Scheduling and Installation

The project is scheduled, materials and equipment are coordinated, and the work is completed with attention to the surrounding property.

04

Communication and Follow-Through

Clients receive clear updates and a final review of the completed work, maintenance considerations, and recommended next steps.
