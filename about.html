About Us

Locally operated and built on trust. Blue Back Landscaping has been serving West Hartford and surrounding communities with professional property care you can count on.

Our Story

A Locally Operated Company You Can Count On

Blue Back Landscaping is a locally operated company serving West Hartford and surrounding areas. With 80 or more clients and growing, we focus on reliable service, clear communication, and consistently clean results.

We started with a simple idea: property care should be easy, predictable, and done right the first time. Every plan we build is designed to take the stress off your plate. One team, one contact, one monthly price.
