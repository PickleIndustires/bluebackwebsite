A Local Company Built Around Reliable Property Care

Locally operated and built on trust. Blue Back Landscaping serves West Hartford and Greater Hartford with organized property care for residential, estate, HOA, and commercial clients.

Our Story

A Locally Operated Company You Can Count On

Blue Back Landscaping began with a focus on dependable residential property care and has grown into a team serving recurring residential clients, private estates, HOAs, and commercial properties. With 80 or more clients and growing, we now combine organized maintenance with drainage, grading, landscape renovations, walkways, seasonal work, and long-term property improvements.

Every project and every recurring route is built on the same idea: property care should be organized, well communicated, and done right the first time. One team, clear scheduling, and consistent standards, whether the work is a single afternoon or an ongoing managed property.
