Services

From weekly lawn maintenance to snow and ice management, Blue Back Landscaping provides reliable, full-service property care for residential and commercial clients throughout the season.

All Services

Seasonal Maintenance

Our Seasonal Maintenance Package is built for clients who want everything handled with one team and one predictable monthly price. From mowing and trimming to mulch, cleanups, and more, we keep your property consistently sharp all season.

Snow & Ice Management

Reliable snow and ice management for residential and commercial properties. We provide plowing, clearing, and deicing with proactive service, plus email and SMS updates so you're always informed during storms.

Drainage Solutions

We diagnose and correct drainage issues to protect your property and prevent water damage. Solutions include grading adjustments, French drains, and runoff management tailored to your site.

Seasonal Decor

Enhance your property with clean, professional seasonal plantings and decor. We install and maintain arrangements that elevate curb appeal throughout the year.

Shrub Maintenance

Detailed trimming and maintenance to keep shrubs clean, healthy, and well-shaped. We focus on proper pruning techniques that promote long-term growth and a polished appearance.

Grading & Hardscaping

From regrading to improve drainage to installing walkways, stone, and outdoor features, we deliver functional and visually clean results that elevate your property.
