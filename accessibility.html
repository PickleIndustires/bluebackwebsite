Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to an inclusive experience for everyone.

Last updated: April 29, 2026

Our Commitment

Blue Back Landscaping LLC is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to all people, regardless of ability or technology. We believe everyone deserves equal access to information about our services, and we strive to meet or exceed the requirements of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

Measures We Have Taken

To support accessibility, we have incorporated the following practices throughout this website:

  • Keyboard navigation — all interactive elements are reachable and operable using a keyboard alone.
  • Skip link — a "Skip to main content" link is provided at the top of every page to allow keyboard and screen reader users to bypass repetitive navigation.
  • Descriptive alt text — all meaningful images include descriptive alternative text. Decorative images are marked appropriately so screen readers skip them.
  • Color contrast — text and interactive elements meet or exceed WCAG 2.1 AA contrast ratio requirements.
  • Semantic HTML — pages are built with proper heading hierarchy, landmark regions (header, main, nav, footer), and ARIA attributes where appropriate.
  • Responsive design — the site adapts to a wide range of screen sizes and zoom levels without loss of content or functionality.
  • Focus indicators — visible focus styles are provided for all interactive elements so keyboard users can always see where they are on the page.
  • No content that flashes — we do not use content that flashes more than three times per second, which could trigger photosensitive reactions.

Known Limitations

We are continuously working to improve accessibility across the site. Some third-party content or linked resources may not fully meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards, as they are outside our direct control. If you encounter any barrier, please let us know and we will do our best to provide an alternative.

Feedback and Contact

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website. If you experience any difficulty accessing content, or if you believe any part of our site does not meet applicable accessibility standards, please contact us:

  • Email: info@bluebacklandscaping.com
  • Phone: (860) 735-4145

We aim to respond to accessibility feedback within 5 business days and will work with you to provide information or services through alternative means if needed.

Formal Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response to your accessibility concern, you may contact the relevant government body in your jurisdiction. In the United States, you may file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Justice regarding Title III of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

Technical Specifications

This website relies on the following technologies for conformance with WCAG 2.1 AA:

  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • JavaScript (ECMAScript 2015+)
  • WAI-ARIA 1.1

Assessment Approach

Blue Back Landscaping LLC assesses the accessibility of this website through self-evaluation. We test using keyboard-only navigation and review our markup against WCAG 2.1 AA criteria during development.
