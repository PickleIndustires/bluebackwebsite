Drainage & Grading

Drainage and Grading Solutions for Problem Areas

Standing water, concentrated runoff, erosion, and uneven grades can damage lawns, planting beds, walkways, and structures. Blue Back evaluates visible site conditions and installs practical drainage and grading improvements suited to the property.

Request a Drainage Consultation

Problems We Help Address

  • Standing water
  • Downspout runoff
  • Erosion channels
  • Water moving toward structures
  • Wet or unusable lawn areas
  • Uneven or settled ground
  • Failed lawn areas
  • Poor surface drainage

Potential Solutions

  • Surface grading adjustments
  • French drains where appropriate
  • Catch basins and solid-pipe drainage where appropriate
  • Downspout extensions
  • Runoff routing
  • Swales
  • Soil preparation
  • Lawn restoration
  • Erosion-resistant landscape improvements
The appropriate solution depends on slope, soil, runoff volume, utilities, discharge location, and surrounding conditions.

Our Process

01

Site Review

We walk the property to see how water moves and where it collects.

Frequently Asked Questions

A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with a perforated pipe that collects and redirects water moving through the soil. A catch basin is a surface grate that collects standing water at a low point and routes it through solid pipe. Many properties use a combination of both, depending on where the water is coming from.
