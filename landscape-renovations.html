Landscape Renovations

Practical Landscape Renovations That Improve the Property

A landscape does not always need to be completely replaced. Blue Back renovates overgrown, outdated, damaged, or underused areas with improvements designed around the property’s appearance, maintenance needs, and long-term use.

Discuss a Landscape Renovation

What We Renovate

  • Garden bed creation and renovation
  • Shrub and plant installation
  • Mulch and decorative stone
  • Bed edging
  • Removal of overgrown or failed material
  • Native and lower-maintenance planting approaches where appropriate
  • Woodland and naturalized-area improvements
  • Seasonal planting
  • Lawn and bed transitions
  • Property cleanup before installation

Good Fit for This Service

  • Aging front foundation beds
  • Overgrown landscape areas
  • Empty or unfinished spaces
  • Properties needing improved curb appeal
  • Natural areas requiring a more organized plan
  • Clients seeking phased improvements
